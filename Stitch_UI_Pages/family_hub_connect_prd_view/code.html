# PRD: Family Hub Connect - The All-in-One Family Organizer ## 1. Executive Summary Family Hub Connect is a comprehensive mobile-first web application designed to streamline family management through organization, communication, financial education, and creative play. It leverages AI to provide smart assistance and personalized experiences. ## 2. Target Audience * **Parents:** Seeking a unified tool to manage household logistics, chores, and family schedules. * **Children/Teens:** Engaging with a gamified system for chores, learning financial literacy, and exploring creative AI tools. ## 3. Core Modules & Key Features ### A. Family Organization & Planning * **Central Dashboard:** Daily summary, chores, and updates. * **Chore Management:** Assignments, photo verification, and AI suggestions. * **Gamified Rewards:** Point-based system with badges and "Mystery Boxes". * **Shared Calendar & Shopping Lists:** Collaborative planning with Google integration. ### B. Communication & Connection * **Family Messaging & Shout-Outs:** Private/group chats and a praise board. * **Family Timeline:** A digital scrapbook of memories and milestones. * **Family Constitution:** Shared values and house rules. ### C. Finance & Education * **Digital Wallets & Allowance:** Automated tracking of kids' funds. * **Savings Goals & Family Bank:** Tools for teaching financial responsibility. * **Screen Time Bank:** Earning and spending digital time. ### D. Creative & Fun Zone * **Creator's Studio:** AI Avatar Creator, Story Generator, and Digital Drawing Board. * **Movie Night Picker:** Collaborative lists with a random selection wheel. ### E. Home & Life Management * **Pet Hub & Vehicle Maintenance:** Tracking logs and schedules. * **Digital Vault:** Secure storage for important documents. * **Family Care Hub:** Health logs and general health info (AI Nurse-Bot). ## 4. Visual Identity & UX Principles * **Warm & Accessible:** Use a vibrant but soft color palette that feels inviting for all ages. * **Modular & Customizable:** Allow users to personalize their dashboard and navigation. * **Clear Information Hierarchy:** High-level summaries on the dashboard with easy drill-downs. * **Gamification Elements:** Use badges, progress bars, and playful icons to encourage engagement. ## 5. Technology Focus * **AI Integration:** Powered by Gemini for chores, stories, and health info. * **Third-Party Sync:** Google Calendar and Email integration. * **Security:** Password-protected digital vault for sensitive data.
