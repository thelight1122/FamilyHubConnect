## 1. Getting Started

### Signing In

1. Open the app — you'll land on the **Sign In** screen.
2. Enter your family email address and password.
3. Tap **Sign In**.

> **Demo mode:** Use `david@thompson.family` with any password to explore the app with sample Thompson family data.

You can also tap **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple** for social sign-in (coming soon).

New family? Tap **Create Account** to begin the onboarding flow and set up your family's values, rules, and member list.

### Finding Your Way Around

At the bottom of the screen you'll always see the **navigation bar** with five tabs:

| Tab | What it does |

| **Home** | Your personal dashboard |
| **Finance** | Family money, savings, and tools |
| **Chores** | Your tasks and rewards store |
| **Sports** | Team schedules and chat |
| **More** | Health, pets, governance, and settings |

Some tabs show a small red badge — that means there's something waiting for your attention (like an unread message or a pending appeal).

## 2. Home Dashboard

The Dashboard is your personalised home base. Everything important for the day is here.

### What You'll See

- **Greeting** — A personalised welcome (e.g., "Good morning, Leo!")
- **Points Balance** — Your current points total and this week's allowance
- **Today's Schedule** — Your next few events with times (practices, meals, activities)
- **Chores Progress** — A bar showing how many of today's tasks are done
- **Quick Actions** — Tap any tile to jump straight to Finance, Sports, Pet Hub, or Family Court
- **Shout-Out** — A recognition card celebrating a recent family win

### Notifications

Tap the **bell icon** in the top-right corner to see recent alerts:

- Pending appeals waiting for review
- Chores that family members have completed
- Practice or event schedule changes

Tap the **×** to close the panel.

## 3. Chores & Rewards

Tap the **Chores** tab at the bottom to open your tasks and rewards.

### My Tasks

This is your to-do list for the day. Each task shows:

- The task name and due date
- How many points you'll earn for completing it
- A circle checkbox on the left

**To complete a task:** Tap the circle on the left. It turns green and the task name gets a strikethrough. Your completion count and progress bar update immediately.

**Tasks that need a photo:** Some tasks have a camera icon and a **"Verify 📷"** button. This means you need to take a photo as proof.

1. Tap **Verify 📷**.
2. Your device's camera opens.
3. Take a photo of the completed task.
4. A confirmation message appears: *"Photo submitted — task verified!"* and the task is marked complete.

### Rewards Store

Tap **Rewards Store** at the top to switch tabs.

Here you can spend your points on rewards like:

- Extra Screen Time
- Movie Night
- Pizza Night
- Skip a Chore

Each reward shows how many points it costs. When you have enough points, the **Redeem** button lights up — tap it to claim your reward!

## 4. Finance Hub

Tap **Finance** at the bottom to see the family's financial picture.

 What You'll See

- **Family Savings Card** — The total amount saved by everyone, plus how much it's grown this month
- **AI Tip** — A helpful financial tip for the family
- **Finance Tools** — Tap any tool to explore it:
  - **Allowance** — Manage weekly allowances for each family member
  - **Savings Goals** — Set and track savings targets
  - **Family Bank** — Request and manage family loans
  - **Market Sim** — Practice investing with a simulated stock market

### Member Wallets

Scroll down to see everyone's current wallet balance shown as a bar. The longer the bar, the more they have saved relative to the family maximum.

### Recent Activity

The transaction list at the bottom shows money coming in and going out:

- **Green +$** amounts — money earned or deposited
- **Red -$** amounts — money spent or withdrawn

## 5. Market Simulator

The Market Simulator lets you practice investing without using real money — great for learning how stocks work!

### Reading Your Portfolio

At the top you'll see:

- Your current portfolio value (e.g., $3,420.50)
- Today's change (e.g., +2.4%)
- A 7-day chart showing how your portfolio has moved over the past week — peaks mean the value went up, dips mean it went down

### Buying and Selling Stocks

Three companies are listed below the chart:

- Each shows the current price and today's change percentage
- Tap **Buy** to add shares to your portfolio (uses virtual money)
- Tap **Sell** to sell shares you own

> This is all simulated — no real money is involved. It's a safe place to learn and experiment!

## 6. Family Bank (Loans)

The Family Bank lets children request loans from the family with 0% interest (no extra fees — you only pay back what you borrowed).

### Viewing Active Loans

Your current loans are shown with:

- What the loan is for (e.g., Mountain Bike)
- The total amount borrowed
- How much you've paid back so far
- A progress bar showing how close you are to paying it off
- Your weekly repayment amount

### Requesting a New Loan

1. Scroll down to **Request New Loan**
2. Enter the amount you need
3. Describe what it's for in the **Purpose** field
4. Tap **Submit** — a parent will review your request

### Loan History

Past loans that have been fully repaid appear in the history section at the bottom.

## 7. Sports & Activities

Tap **Sports** at the bottom to open the Locker Room — your team hub.

### Switching Between Teams

At the top, you'll see a row of team buttons. Tap a team name to switch between family members' sports and activities (e.g., Leo's Soccer or Sarah's Ballet).

### What's on the Team Page

- **Team Card** — Team name, which family member it belongs to, and the next event
- **Schedule** — Upcoming games and practices with date, time, and location. Home games show a house icon; away games show a plane icon.
- **Equipment Checklist** — Everything you need to bring. Items marked with a tick are packed; unticked items still need to be added to your bag.
- **Team Contacts** — Coaches and teammates with their names and roles. Tap **Message** to reach out.
- **Open Team Chat** — Tap to jump straight into the group chat

## 8. Team Chat

Team Chat is a group messaging space for your sports team.

### Reading Messages

- Messages from you appear on the **right** in blue
- Messages from others appear on the **left** in grey with the sender's name
- An **Announcement banner** at the top highlights important upcoming events (e.g., game details, location changes)
- **Green dots** next to avatars at the top show who is currently online

### Sending a Message

1. Tap the message field at the bottom
2. Type your message
3. Tap the **send button** (arrow icon) or press Enter

Tap the **paperclip icon** to attach a photo or file (coming soon).

When someone is typing, you'll see three animated dots below their name.

## 9. Health Logs

Go to **More → Health Logs** to view and manage a family member's health record.

### Health Profile

At the top you'll see the member's profile card:

- Name and age
- Blood type
- Known allergies
- Vaccination status badge (Up to Date / Needs Update)

### Logging a Health Event

1. Tap **Log New Health Event** (the blue button at the top)
2. Choose an event type by tapping one of the pill buttons:
   - Illness
   - Injury
   - Doctor Visit
   - Medication Change
   - Other
3. Type any notes in the **Notes** field (symptoms, treatment, what the doctor said, etc.)
4. Tap **Save Event**
5. A confirmation message appears and the panel closes

### Active Medications

The medications section shows current prescriptions with the medication name, dosage, and how many days remain.

### Health History

Scroll down to see past health events listed in order. Each entry has a coloured dot on the left indicating the event type.

> The app is for personal record-keeping only and does not replace professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical decisions.

## 10. Pet Hub

Go to **More → Pet Hub** to manage Luna's daily care routine.

### Pet Profile

At the top: Luna's photo, name, breed, and a "Healthy" status badge. Below that, three key stats are shown: Weight, Age, and Activity Level.

### Feeding Schedule

The Feeding Schedule shows morning and evening meals. A green tick means the meal has been given; a grey clock means it's still pending. Status updates daily based on scheduled feeding times.

### Daily Walks

Two walks are listed (Morning and Afternoon). Each shows the scheduled time, duration, and distance.

**To mark a walk as done:** Tap the **Pending** badge next to a walk. It turns green and switches to **Done**.

Tap **Done** again to toggle it back to **Pending** if you need to undo it.

### Upcoming Vet Visit

At the bottom, the next vet appointment is shown in a card with:

- The calendar date (month and day number)
- Appointment type (e.g., Annual Check-up)
- Clinic name and time
- A "Reminder set" badge confirming a notification will be sent

## 11. Family Timeline

Go to **More → Family Timeline** to explore your family's shared memory board.

### Browsing Entries

Entries are organised by month, newest first. Each entry has a coloured dot and card:

| Dot Colour | Entry Type | What it Shows |

| Amber | Achievement | A star icon, bold title, and a description of the accomplishment |
| Blue | Memory | An image or photo placeholder with like and comment counts |
| Rose | Journal | An italic quote with the author's name and date |
| Green | Milestone | A milestone icon, title, and date badge |

### Filtering

Tap a filter pill at the top to show only certain types:

- **All** — everything
- **Achievements** — accomplishments and victories
- **Memories** — photos and shared moments
- **Journal** — personal reflections and written entries

If a filter has no entries yet, you'll see an empty state with a friendly message encouraging you to start capturing moments.

## 13. Family Governance

Go to **More → Family Governance** to access the family's dispute resolution and rules management system.

The page is divided into four tabs — tap any tab name to switch:

### Resolution

This is where active mediations happen. Tap **New Resolution Request** to formally raise a dispute or concern. This takes you to the appeal form (see Section 14).

### Jury Pool

Shows which family members are currently assigned as mediators and when their term runs. The active mediator has a green dot next to their name.

### Archive

A history of past rulings — useful for seeing how similar situations were handled before. Each entry shows the case ID, parties involved, outcome, and date.

### Rule Book

A link directly to the Family Constitution for reference during governance discussions.

## 14. Submitting an Appeal (Children)

If you've been given a consequence that you think is unfair, you can formally appeal it.

Go to **More → Submit Appeal** (or tap **New Resolution Request** in Family Governance).

### How to Submit Your Appeal

1. **Review your consequence** — The card at the top shows what consequence is currently active and why it was given.

2. **Fill in Your Reason** — In the **Your Reason** field, explain your side of the story honestly. This field is required.

3. **Suggest an Alternative** (optional) — If you have a fairer consequence in mind, describe it in the **Proposed Alternative** field.

4. **Reflect** (optional) — Use the **What I've Learned** field to show you understand what happened and what you'd do differently.

5. **Attach Evidence** (optional) — Tap the upload area to attach a photo or file that supports your appeal. The filename appears once attached.

6. **Submit** — Tap **Submit Appeal**. The button is only active once you've filled in your reason.

### After Submitting

The form is replaced by a success screen:

- "Appeal Submitted!" confirmation
- A checklist of what happens next (parent reviews → decision within 24 hours → you receive a notification)
- A **Back to More** button to return to the main menu

## 15. Reviewing an Appeal (Parents)

When a child submits an appeal, you'll receive a notification. Go to **More → Review Appeal** to see the details.

### Reading the Appeal

- **Case Summary** — The case ID, child's name, and charge at the top
- **Original Consequence** — Shown in a red card so it's clearly visible
- **Child's Argument** — Their explanation, shown as a quote
- **Proposed Alternative** — Their suggested replacement consequence (if provided)
- **Evidence** — Any attached files they submitted

### Making Your Decision

Scroll down to see three decision buttons:

- **Approve Appeal** (green) — The child's appeal is accepted; the consequence is lifted or changed
- **Deny Appeal** (red outline) — The original consequence stands
- **Open Negotiation** (blue) — You'd like to discuss the situation further before deciding

**Before your decision is finalised**, a confirmation popup appears showing:

- The type of decision you're making
- The child's name who will be notified
- A **Cancel** button (if you tapped the wrong one) and a **Confirm** button

Once confirmed, a result card appears at the bottom of the page confirming the decision and noting that the child has been notified.

### Summary Stats

Three numbers at the top give you a quick overview:

- **Active Measures** — How many consequences are currently running
- **Weekly Resolution** — Percentage of issues resolved this week
- **Trend** — Whether things are improving compared to last week

### Active Consequences

Each active consequence shows:

- A category icon (screen time, chores, etc.)
- The consequence title and what rule was violated
- A status badge: **In Progress** or **Completed**
- A progress bar showing how far through the consequence the child is
- A **Mark Done** button

**To close out a consequence:** Tap **Mark Done**. The status updates immediately and a confirmation message appears.

### Consequence History

Scroll down to see consequences that have been fully resolved, with dates and outcomes.

## 18. Inviting Family Members

Go to **More → Invite Family** (or find it on the More page) to add new members to your family account.

Three ways to invite:

### Share Link

1. Tap the **Share Link** tab
2. Tap **Copy Link**
3. Paste the link into a message app, email, or anywhere else and send it to your family member
4. They open the link and follow the setup steps

### Email

1. Tap the **Email** tab
2. Type the person's email address
3. Tap **Send Invite**
4. They'll receive an email with a join link

### QR Code

1. Tap the **QR Code** tab
2. Show the QR code on your screen to the person you want to invite
3. They scan it with their phone's camera
4. The join link opens automatically

### Pending Invites

Below the invite options, you'll see a list of people who have been invited but haven't joined yet. Each entry shows their name, role (Adult or Child), and when the invite was sent. You can **Resend** the invite or **Cancel** it if needed.

## 19. Tips & Tricks

### Red Badges on the Nav Bar

Small red circles on the **Sports** and **More** tabs mean something needs your attention — like an unread team chat message or a pending appeal to review. The number inside the badge tells you how many items are waiting.

### Earning Points

You earn points by completing chores. Check the point value on each task before you start — tasks worth more points are usually bigger jobs! Photo-verified tasks are worth points too.

### Spending Points Wisely

Before redeeming a reward, check the **Rewards Store** to see everything available and plan which rewards to save up for. Bigger rewards like Movie Night or Pizza Night cost more points but are worth the wait!

### The Points/Rewards Cycle

1. Complete chores → earn points
2. Accumulate enough points
3. Redeem points in the Rewards Store
4. New chores reset daily — there's always a fresh chance to earn

### Appeal Tips (for Children)

- Be honest and specific in your reason — vague answers are less likely to succeed
- Suggest a realistic alternative consequence, not just "nothing"
- Attaching evidence (like a photo or explanation) makes your appeal stronger
- The What I've Learned section shows maturity and responsibility

### Keeping the Pet Schedule on Track

The Pet Hub updates walk statuses in real time — mark walks as Done as soon as they're completed so everyone in the family can see the dog has been walked and doesn't need another trip out.

### Using the Timeline as a Family Scrapbook

Encourage everyone in the family to add entries — achievements (big and small), fun memories, and journal reflections. Over time, the Timeline becomes a treasured record of your family's story.
